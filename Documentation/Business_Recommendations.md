# Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize inventory and promotion planning for high-value products such as Laptop and Tablet.
2. Review products with high quantities but comparatively lower sales values.
3. Investigate Central region performance because it has the highest documented regional sales.
4. Review North region inventory because it has the highest documented quantity.
5. Investigate West region performance because it has the lowest documented regional sales.
6. Use monthly revenue trends for seasonal inventory and promotion planning.
