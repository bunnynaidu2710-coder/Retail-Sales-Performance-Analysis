# Business Recommendations

The recommendations below are based on the supplied 80-row retail dataset.

1. **Prioritize Laptop inventory:** Laptop is the highest-revenue product at **24,643.10**.
2. **Monitor high-volume products:** T-Shirt has the highest quantity at **61 units**, while Laptop, Tablet and Office Chair each also reach 32 units.
3. **Strengthen Central-region performance:** Central produces the highest regional revenue at **15,622.00**.
4. **Investigate East-region volume:** East has the highest regional quantity at **85 units**.
5. **Investigate returns:** There are **19 returned transactions**, representing **16,447.20** of recorded transaction revenue in the supplied data.
6. **Prepare for the year-end peak:** October has the highest monthly revenue at **11,170.65**, followed by November and December. Inventory and promotions can be planned around this pattern.
7. **Focus category strategy on Electronics:** Electronics contributes **39,422.20**, the highest category revenue in the dataset.
8. **Use inventory-after-sale data:** Products with low remaining inventory should be reviewed before campaigns or peak periods.
